What Is a 4Runner Hood Scoop Decal?

Installation Process (Easy DIY Application)

One of the biggest advantages of a 4Runner hood scoop decal is how easy it is to install.

No drilling. No cutting. No modifications.

Just a clean surface and a few minutes of care.

Basic installation steps:

  1. Clean the hood scoop thoroughly (remove dust, wax, and oils)

  2. Dry the surface completely

  3. Align the decal carefully before applying

  4. Press from the center outward to avoid bubbles

  5. Use light pressure to secure edges

Estimated install time: 10–20 minutes

Tips for best results:

  • Install in a shaded or indoor area

  • Avoid applying to a hot surface

  • Use a microfiber cloth or squeegee for smoothing

  • Take your time with alignment before sticking

Even first-time installers can achieve a clean, professional finish. The precision-cut design helps guide placement naturally.
